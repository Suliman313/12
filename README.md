## Summary
Reducing sports fanatism and encouraging fans to support with awareness and objective football analysis.

## 1. Your Idea in a Short Sentence
Reducing sports fanatism and encouraging conscious supporting and objective sports analysis.

## 2. Background
* **The Problem:** A lack of awareness regarding sound and objective sports analysis.
* **Frequency:** The widespread presence of sports fanatism among youth.
* **Personal Motivation:** Mitigating sports fanatism to address a pressing societal issue.

## 3. Data and AI Techniques
* **Data Sources:** Utilizing artificial intelligence models (such as Gemini AI) to analyze sports data, text, and generate awareness content.
* **Techniques Used:** Artificial intelligence (AI) techniques to analyze sports behaviors and provide guidelines for conscious fan behavior.
* **Implementation:** Utilizing AI tools to deliver solutions and ideas that promote sports awareness.

## 4. How It Is Used
* **Context:** Used across social media platforms and sports communities to educate sports fans.
* **Beneficiaries:** Sports fans, youth, and individuals interested in the sports community.

## 5. Challenges
The project does not completely solve the root issue of deep-rooted loyalties, as technology has limitations in changing personal beliefs without individual willingness.

## 6. What's Next?
Developing an interactive application or platform powered by artificial intelligence to evaluate and analyze sports discussions and reward points for conscious fan behavior.
